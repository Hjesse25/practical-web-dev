Simple Bacon and Eggs Recipe

This is an easy/quick recipe that makes light and fluffy scrambled eggs along with delicious crispy bacon.

Preparation Time

  • Total: 30 Minutes
  • Preparation: 5 Minutes
  • Cooking: 25 Minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 strips of thick cut bacon
  • 2-3 eggs
  • 1oz milk
  • 1 Kraft Single American
  • 20 grams Shredded Mild Cheddar Cheese
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook Bacon: Preheat your oven to 400 degrees and place 2-3 strips of thick cut bacon on baking sheet lined with aluminum foil. Cook bacon for 15-20 minutes until crispy. Cook bacon and take it out of the oven before cooking your eggs.
  2. Prepare Eggs: Crack open 2-3 eggs in a bowl. Add a splash of milk to the eggs. Scramble the eggs with a fork until they area light yellow liquid.
  3. Cook Eggs: Heat a small to medium sized pan to medium heat. Pour bacon grease into the pan and swish it around until the entire bottom of the pan is coated. Turn the oven burner to low heat and pour your scrambled eggs into the pan.
  4. Add Ingredients to the Eggs: Add 3 shakes of pepper, 1 slice of Kraft american cheese, 15g of shredded medium cheddar cheese, and your backon into the egg liquid in the pan. It's best to tear the bacon and Kraft cheese into small pieces before adding it to the pan.
  5. Stir the Eggs: Turn the oven burner back to medium heat and stir your bacon, egg, and cheese mixture until your scrambled eggs become firm and fully cooked.
  6. Optional: Toast up a fresh loaf of bread or a bagal to go with your scrambled eggs and bacon.
  7. Eat! Enjoy your meal!
