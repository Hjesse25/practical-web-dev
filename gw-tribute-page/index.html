George Washington

1st President of the United States

Early Life

  • Born in 1732 in the colony of Virginia
  • Learned to be a trained draftsman and map maker at a young age
  • Became a professional land surveyor in 1749

Military Career

  • Joined the Virginia militia in 1752
  • Lead a group of over 300 militia and Native Americans in the French and Indian War where he was victorious
  • Won several battles and honed his leadership skills through 1758

Political Life

  • Served in various political positions in Virginia in the 1760s
  • Became the Commander in Chief of the Army during the Revolutionary war (1775-1783)
  • Served as the first president of the United States from 1789-1797
  • Passed away in 1799
